About

Why a catalogue, and not another chat app

Overview

A centralized place for academic doubts

Student Doubt Forum is a frontend prototype demonstrating how a dedicated academic platform could work. Instead of doubts scattered across group chats and forgotten screenshots, every question here is filed as a structured card — subject, teacher, description — and stays in a searchable archive once it's answered. This build focuses entirely on the interface: layout, navigation, and interaction patterns, built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Objectives

What this prototype sets out to show

01

Design an intuitive interface for academic discussion.

02

Demonstrate the full workflow of asking and answering doubts.

03

Give students and teachers a clear, organized layout each.

04

Build a responsive, modern site across desktop and mobile.

Benefits

Why this beats a scattered chat thread

Nothing gets lost

Answered doubts stay in the archive instead of scrolling out of a chat.

Clear ownership

Every doubt is addressed to a specific teacher, so nothing falls through.

Searchable by subject

Category filters mean past answers are easy to find again.

One format for every doubt

Title, subject, description — consistent structure, faster answers.
